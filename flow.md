# Item Return Desk - App Flow

## What This App Does

Item Return Desk is a mobile application that helps people find their lost items or return items they have found. Think of it as a digital lost and found board for your campus or community.

---

## Getting Started

### First Time Users

1. **Welcome Screen**: When you first open the app, you see a welcome screen with the app logo
2. **Introduction Slides**: The app shows you a few slides explaining how it works
3. **Create Account**: You need to sign up with your name, email, and password
4. **Login**: If you already have an account, you can log in directly

---

## Main Features

### Home Screen

After logging in, you land on the **Home Screen** which shows:

- **Welcome Message**: A personalized greeting with your name
- **Statistics Cards**: 
  - How many lost items are currently posted
  - How many found items are currently posted
  - How many posts you have created
- **Quick Access Cards**: 
  - "Lost Items" - Browse all items people have lost
  - "Found Items" - Browse all items people have found
- **Recent Posts**: A list of the most recent posts (from last 24 hours or 7 days)
- **Quick Tips**: Helpful suggestions like adding clear photos, being specific about location, etc.
- **Create Post Button**: A floating "+" button to create a new post

---

## Creating a Post

### When You Lose Something

1. Tap the **"+" button** on the Home Screen
2. Select **"Lost"** as the post type
3. Fill in the details:
   - **Title**: What did you lose? (e.g., "Black iPhone 12")
   - **Description**: More details about the item
   - **Category**: Choose from Electronics, ID Cards, Accessories, Clothing, Books, or Other
   - **Location**: Where did you lose it? (e.g., "Library, 2nd floor")
   - **Reward/Tip** (Optional): You can offer a reward to encourage people to help find it
   - **Photos**: Add up to 5 photos of the item
   - **Videos**: Add up to 2 short videos (max 60 seconds)
4. Tap **"Create Post"**
5. Your post is now live and visible to everyone

### When You Find Something

1. Tap the **"+" button** on the Home Screen
2. Select **"Found"** as the post type
3. Fill in the details:
   - **Title**: What did you find? (e.g., "Blue Backpack")
   - **Description**: Describe what you found
   - **Category**: Choose the appropriate category
   - **Location**: Where did you find it?
   - **Photos**: Add photos of the found item
   - **Videos**: Add videos if needed
4. Tap **"Create Post"**
5. Your post is now live and people who lost similar items can contact you

---

## Browsing Posts

### Viewing Lost Items

1. From Home Screen, tap **"Lost Items"** card
2. You see a list of all lost items posted by others
3. You can:
   - **Search**: Type keywords to find specific items
   - **Filter**: Filter by category, location, or time
   - **Tap any post**: View full details

### Viewing Found Items

1. From Home Screen, tap **"Found Items"** card
2. You see a list of all found items posted by others
3. Same search and filter options as Lost Items

---

## Viewing Post Details

When you tap on any post, you see:

- **Photos/Videos**: All images and videos of the item
- **Post Type Badge**: Shows if it's "Lost" or "Found"
- **Category Badge**: What category it belongs to
- **Title**: The post title
- **Description**: Full description of the item
- **Location**: Where it was lost or found
- **Reward**: If a reward is offered (for lost items)
- **Posted By**: Who created this post
- **Time**: When it was posted

### If It's Not Your Post

- **Contact Button**: Tap to start a conversation with the post owner
- This opens a chat where you can message them directly

---

## Chatting with Others

### Starting a Conversation

1. When viewing someone's post, tap **"Contact"**
2. A chat screen opens automatically
3. Type your message and send
4. The other person will receive a notification

### Chat Features

- **Message History**: See all previous messages
- **Real-time Updates**: Messages appear instantly
- **Time Stamps**: See when messages were sent
- **Easy Navigation**: Your messages appear on the right, theirs on the left

### Accessing All Chats

1. Tap the **"My Chats"** tab at the bottom
2. See all your conversations in one place
3. Unread messages are highlighted
4. Tap any conversation to continue chatting

---

## Managing Your Posts

### My Posts Screen

1. Tap the **"My Posts"** tab at the bottom
2. See all posts you have created
3. Each post shows:
   - Status (Active, Claimed, Resolved, Archived)
   - Type (Lost or Found)
   - Title and description
   - Location
   - Reward (if applicable)

### Actions on Your Posts

- **Search**: Search through your posts
- **Filter**: Filter by category, type, status, or sort by date
- **Edit**: Tap "Edit" to update your post details
- **Delete**: Tap "Delete" to remove your post permanently
- **View Details**: Tap the post card to see full details

---

## Notifications

### Activity Tab

1. Tap the **"Activity"** tab at the bottom
2. See all your notifications:
   - **New Messages**: When someone messages you
   - **Matches**: When someone finds a similar item
   - **Claims**: When someone claims your found item
   - **Contact Requests**: When someone wants to contact you
   - **Admin Notifications**: Important updates from admins

### Notification Features

- **Unread Badge**: Red number shows unread notifications
- **Mark as Read**: Tap a notification to mark it read
- **Mark All Read**: Button to clear all notifications
- **Direct Navigation**: Tap notification to go to related post or chat

---

## Your Profile

### Profile Screen

1. Tap the **"Profile"** tab at the bottom
2. See your profile information:
   - **Profile Picture**: Your avatar
   - **Name and Email**: Your account details
   - **Statistics**: 
     - Total posts you've created
     - Lost items posted
     - Found items posted
   - **Recent Posts**: Your 3 most recent posts
   - **Account Info**: Email, member since date, account status

### Profile Actions

- **Edit Profile**: Update your name, phone, or profile picture
- **Settings**: Access app settings (tap the gear icon)
- **Change Password**: Update your password
- **View Statistics**: See detailed activity stats

---

## Settings

From Profile Screen, tap the **gear icon** to access:

- **Theme**: Switch between light and dark mode
- **Notifications**: Control notification preferences
- **Privacy**: Manage privacy settings
- **About**: App information and version
- **Logout**: Sign out of your account

---

## Admin Features

If you're an admin user:

- **Admin Panel**: Access from Profile Screen
- **Manage Posts**: View, edit, or remove any post
- **User Management**: View and manage all users
- **Statistics**: See overall app statistics
- **Reports**: Handle reported posts

## Complete User Journey Examples

### Scenario 1: You Lost Your Phone

1. Open the app and log in
2. Tap the "+" button on Home Screen
3. Select "Lost" post type
4. Enter: "iPhone 12 Pro Max - Black"
5. Describe: "Lost my iPhone with a black case, has a crack on the screen"
6. Select category: "Electronics"
7. Enter location: "Cafeteria, near entrance"
8. Add reward: "$50"
9. Upload 2-3 photos of your phone
10. Tap "Create Post"
11. Your post is now live
12. Wait for notifications - someone might message you if they found it
13. When someone contacts you, chat with them to verify it's your phone
14. Once you get it back, you can mark the post as "Resolved"

### Scenario 2: You Found a Wallet

1. Open the app and log in
2. Tap the "+" button on Home Screen
3. Select "Found" post type
4. Enter: "Brown Leather Wallet"
5. Describe: "Found a brown leather wallet with cards inside"
6. Select category: "Accessories"
7. Enter location: "Parking lot, near Building A"
8. Upload a photo of the wallet (without showing personal info)
9. Tap "Create Post"
10. Your post is now live
11. Someone who lost a wallet might contact you
12. Chat with them to verify it's theirs
13. Arrange a meeting place to return it
14. Once returned, mark the post as "Resolved"

### Scenario 3: You See a Lost Item That Might Be Yours

1. Browse "Lost Items" from Home Screen
2. Use search or filters to find similar items
3. Tap on a post that looks like your item
4. Read the full description and check photos
5. If it matches, tap "Contact" button
6. Send a message: "Hi, I think this might be mine. Can you describe [specific detail]?"
7. Wait for their response
8. If confirmed, arrange to meet and verify
9. Once verified, you can claim the item

## Key Benefits

- **Easy to Use**: Simple interface, no technical knowledge needed
- **Fast Communication**: Direct chat with post owners
- **Photo Support**: Visual identification with multiple photos
- **Location Based**: Know exactly where items were lost/found
- **Reward System**: Optional rewards encourage people to help
- **Real-time Updates**: Get notified instantly when someone contacts you
- **Privacy**: Contact through app without sharing personal phone numbers initially
- **Organized**: Categories and filters help you find what you're looking for quickly

---

## Tips for Best Results

1. **Add Clear Photos**: Good quality photos help others identify items
2. **Be Specific**: Detailed descriptions increase chances of finding matches
3. **Update Location**: Be as specific as possible about where item was lost/found
4. **Respond Quickly**: Reply to messages promptly to maintain interest
5. **Mark as Resolved**: Update post status when item is found/returned
6. **Use Categories**: Correct categorization helps people find your post
7. **Offer Rewards**: For valuable items, offering a reward can help

---

## Summary

Item Return Desk connects people who have lost items with people who have found items. It's like a community notice board, but digital and much more powerful. You can post what you lost or found, browse what others have posted, chat directly with people, and get your items back or help return items to their owners.

The app makes the process of finding lost items or returning found items simple, fast, and organized.
